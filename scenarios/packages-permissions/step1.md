# Step 1: Club Authentication & Gate Verification

Before starting your Week 2 tasks, your terminal must be authenticated with the Jarvis Club server.

### Task:
Ensure you completed the authentication gate prompt in the interactive terminal on the right:
1. Provide your registered **Student Roll Number**.
2. Provide your **Lab Passkey** from the [Jarvis Club Labs page](https://jarvis-linux-club.vercel.app/labs).

If you need to re-authenticate or check your current session state:
```bash
club-gate status
```

Click **Verify** below once your terminal prompt opens.
