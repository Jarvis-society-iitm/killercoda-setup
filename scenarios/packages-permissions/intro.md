# IITM Jarvis Linux & Systems Club — Week 2 Lab

Welcome to **Week 2: Packages, Permissions & Environment Variables**!

In this lab, you will put into practice the core systems administration concepts from Session 1 & 2:
1. **Package Management with APT**: Searching, inspecting, and installing system tools.
2. **Numeric and Symbolic Permissions**: Applying exact octal modes (`755`, `700`, `775`, `750`) across a multi-tier permission audit structure.
3. **Environment & PATH Customization**: Building custom scripts and making your custom script directory persistent in `$PATH` across subshells via `~/.bashrc`.
