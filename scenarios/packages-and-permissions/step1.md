# Step 1: Welcome & Club Authentication

Every student lab session in the Jarvis Linux Club is verified cryptographically to bind your interactive progress to your student leaderboard profile.

### Tasks:
1. Look at the terminal on the right. If the login prompt is visible, enter:
   - **Student ID / Roll Number**: Your official IITM roll number (e.g. `25f300xxxx` or your registered ID).
   - **Verification Passkey**: Your personal passkey from the club portal ([https://jarvis-linux-club.vercel.app/labs](https://jarvis-linux-club.vercel.app/labs)).
2. If your terminal did not prompt automatically, run:
   ```bash
   club-gate login
   ```
3. Once you see `[SUCCESS] Verified!`, click the **Check** button below to complete this step.
