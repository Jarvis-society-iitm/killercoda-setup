# Week 1 Session I: The Map, The Vehicle, and The Drive
### IITM Jarvis Linux & Systems Club

Welcome to your first hands-on Linux interactive lab session!

### 🎯 Session Goals
1. Verify and establish communication with your Linux shell and kernel.
2. Master absolute and relative paths, navigating through the single-root filesystem hierarchy.
3. Perform essential file and folder operations (`mkdir`, `touch`, `cp`, `mv`).
4. Inspect files using the right viewing tool (`cat`, `less`, `head`, `tail`).
5. Practice safe deletion and complete the **Mini-Lab Synthesis Challenge**.
