# IITM Jarvis Linux & Systems Club

Welcome to the **Introduction to Linux & Systems** interactive lab!

In this lab, you will get hands-on experience navigating the Linux shell, configuring environment variables, and working with file permissions.

### Prerequisites & Verification
Because XP and leaderboard ranks are tracked on the [Jarvis Club Portal](https://jarvis-linux-club.vercel.app), this environment requires authentication:

1. Look at your interactive terminal on the right.
2. Enter your **Student Roll / ID** (e.g., `21f1001230`).
3. Enter your **Verification Passkey** from your web dashboard.
4. Once verified, the environment will unlock and grant access to the steps!
