# Step 1: Club Authentication & Verification

Before executing commands, your terminal environment must be authenticated with the Jarvis Linux Club backend.

### Task:
1. Complete the prompt on the terminal to the right:
   - **Student ID**: Enter your roll number (e.g. `25f3002130`).
   - **Passkey**: Enter your secure passkey from your club dashboard.
2. If your prompt did not open automatically or you need to inspect your authentication state:
   ```bash
   club-gate status
   ```
3. Test your shell communication as covered in Slide 4:
   ```bash
   echo "Hello Linux"
   ```

Click **Verify** below once authenticated to unlock Step 2.
